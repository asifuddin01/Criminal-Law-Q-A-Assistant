Summary
========================================================================
Adding a 376-row document and amending one section each cost only the
embeddings they actually required. The full build of 621 chunks took 20.5s;
the single-section amendment took under a second.

Chunks are matched on a stable id and compared on a hash of their text, so
a source republished with one provision changed re-embeds one provision.
